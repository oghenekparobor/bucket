# Bucket Vault — internal security review

Independent, pre-audit review of the on-chain program. Not a substitute for an external audit.

- **Reviewer:** independent review agent (Claude), commissioned by the repo owner; not a human auditor
- **Date:** 2026-09-21
- **Program:** `bucket_vault` (`programs/bucket_vault/src/`), Anchor 0.32.1, `overflow-checks = true`, LTO fat.
- **Also reviewed (briefly):** `mock_swap` (`programs/mock_swap/src/lib.rs`) — devnet-only venue, **must never ship to mainnet** (it mints unlimited mock USDC and mock stock; the `swap`/`swap_stocks`/`faucet` instructions have no economic guard beyond a price/fee). Its only relevance to mainnet security is as the CPI target the vault's swap guard is tested against.
- **Program ID:** `GwTYDwQh9nCACjBD44EK5qm5QmfWUw1XtF1RfyrigN29`

## Scope and method

Line-by-line reading of every instruction, the shared vault/CPI/math modules, account contexts and constraints, and the `state`/`errors`/`events` definitions, checked against the product promise:

> Neither the creator nor the keeper (nor the price authority, nor any third party) can move a backer's money out of a vault; mints and redeems never dilute existing holders; commission is exactly 20% of unit-price growth above the high-water mark; redeem never depends on the keeper, API, creator or pool.

Method:

1. Solana checklist walk (signer/owner/discriminator/PDA/`has_one`/remaining-accounts/arithmetic/CPI/reentrancy/reinit/close/Token-2022/price/economic/state-consistency), per instruction.
2. Adversarial modelling of the swap CPI: what an **allow-listed but adversarially-parameterized** swap (Jupiter with keeper-chosen accounts/data, or a Token-2022 mint with a permanent delegate / hooks) can do while signed by the order PDA or the bucket PDA.
3. Every candidate issue was pushed to a proof: a LiteSVM test in `vault/tests/src/review.test.ts` that either **demonstrates the bad outcome** (a finding) or **confirms the defence holds** (documents a non-issue).

Test evidence:
- New: `vault/tests/src/review.test.ts` — 6 tests, all green (`cd vault/tests && npx vitest run src/review.test.ts`).
- Existing suite unchanged and green: **68** LiteSVM tests (`vault/tests`), **10** Rust unit tests (`cargo test -p bucket_vault --lib`).
- No program source or existing test was modified.

## Summary

No critical or high-severity vulnerability was found. The core custody promise holds: **no instruction lets the creator, keeper, price authority or any third party move vault assets anywhere except back into the vault or to the redeeming holder**, and the balance-delta + slippage checks make this robust even against an adversarially-parameterized allow-listed swap. The program is well-constructed: conservative (min-of-spot-and-TWAP) valuation, reference-price + `min()` anti-dilution, price-independent redeem with a keeper-free in-kind exit, a strict swap-account guard, and `overflow-checks` on.

Findings are one **Medium** (an economic/gating invariant, not a custody break), one **Low**, several **Info/accepted trade-offs**, and a set of **centralization/issuer trust** residuals for the external audit.

Counts: Critical 0 · High 0 · Medium 1 · Low 2 · Info 5.

## Findings

| ID | Sev | Instruction | Description | Exploit scenario | Proof | Recommended fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | **Medium** | `open_mint` / `fill_mint` | The "creator must fund first, ≥ `min_creator_deposit` ($25)" gate is satisfied by *opening* a ≥$25 order and filling **one dust leg** (`creator_funded` flips on the first leg fill with `qty>0`, regardless of amount). The creator can then refund the rest. Because every later mint follows the vault's **current value proportions**, the first real backer's whole deposit is then routed into the single dust-funded holding instead of the advertised recipe. | Creator opens $25, fills 2¢ of the NVDAx leg (→ `creator_funded=true`), closes/refunds ~$24.93. Vault now holds ~2¢ of one of three recipe holdings. A backer mints $1,000; the order budgets ~100% into NVDAx (0 to AAPLx/pSPACEX). Skin-in-the-game gate defeated; first backer over-concentrated and must be rebalanced at swap cost. No theft and no dilution (backer still gets tokens worth their money). | `review.test.ts` → `F1 creator-funding gate is bypassable with a dust fill` (2 tests: `creator_funded` flips on a 2¢ fill with vault value `< $1` in one holding; backer's $1,000 order budgets `>$990` to NVDAx, `0` to the others). | Require the creator's funding threshold to be met by **filled value**, not by opening an order: e.g. gate non-creator mints on `bucket.last_value_e6 >= min_creator_deposit_e6` (settled) rather than on a boolean flipped by any fill; and/or only flip `creator_funded` once the creator order is fully filled/closed with cumulative spend ≥ minimum. Optionally cap how far a single mint may deviate from target weights. |
| F2 | Info (accepted) | `redeem` | A `redeem` skips commission settlement when **any** holding price is stale (`settled=false`). A holder who exits while the feed is stale realizes their gains and pays **zero** commission; the creator/platform never collect the 20% on that growth. | With a held +25% gain (unit price > HWM, commission due on a fresh settle), the price feed lapses > `max_price_age_secs` (1h). The holder redeems: `settled=false`, no fee tokens minted, holder gets full pro-rata of the appreciated vault. Holders **cannot force** staleness, so this only bites during a genuine feed outage — but then it is an unbounded commission leak for whoever exits. | `review.test.ts` → `F2 a stale-price redeem escapes commission entirely` (gain held through TWAP window so commission is due; after feed goes stale, `Redeemed.settled==false` and both fee accounts stay `0`, while the redeem still reserves the full pro-rata slice). | Documented and accepted (docs/decisions.md #2/#4: redeem must never depend on the feed). If commission capture matters, consider settling on the **last fresh** conservative valuation when spot is stale but a recent settlement exists, capped so it can never block or reduce the holder's slice. Operationally: keep the price feed's uptime SLA tight and alert on staleness. |

## Per-instruction checklist results

Legend: OK = checked, no issue. Notes call out what enforces it or any caveat.

### admin: `initialize_config`, `update_config`, `set_roles`, `add_asset`, `set_asset_status`, `update_price`, `force_price`
- **Signers/authority:** `initialize_config` gated to the program **upgrade authority** (`programdata.upgrade_authority == authority`), init-once (PDA `["config"]`). `update_config`/`set_roles`/`add_asset`/`set_asset_status` gated `address = config.admin`. `update_price` gated `signer == config.price_authority`; `force_price` gated `signer == config.admin`. OK (existing tests cover unauthorized paths).
- **Params validation:** `validate_params` bounds commission ≤ 50%, fees ≤ 5%, weights whole-percent and ordered, slippage ≤ 10%, positive TTL/age/window. OK.
- **`add_asset`:** Asset PDA `["asset", mint]`, `token_program`/`decimals` copied from the real mint (`InterfaceAccount<Mint>`), `price>0`, `extra_cost ≤ 5%`. Assets are **only** creatable by admin and uniquely keyed by mint — this is what makes the `Account::try_from` reads in `load_holdings` safe (see state-consistency). OK.
- **`update_price` clamp/TWAP:** push clamped to ±`max_price_move_bps` of TWAP, TWAP moves toward the push over the window. Prevents a single bad print moving valuation. OK.
- **`force_price` (admin, unbounded):** sets price and resets TWAP. Centralization risk R1 — a compromised admin can inflate valuation and mint fake commission (dilution), but cannot withdraw vault assets. Info.
- **decimals arithmetic:** `value_e6` computes `10^decimals`; an asset with `decimals ≥ 39` would panic (`u128` overflow) — admin-gated and unrealistic (real assets are 8–9 dp), and `overflow-checks` makes it an abort, not a wraparound. Info (I3).

### lifecycle: `create_bucket`, `update_bucket_info`, `close_bucket`, `settle_commission`, `claim_fees`
- **`create_bucket`:** creator signs; `id == creator_state.next_bucket_id`; `active_buckets < max`; recipe validated (`recipe::validate`: count, unique mints, whole-percent weights within per-type caps, sum 100%, allowed+enabled mints, canonical vault ATA per mint). Bucket/mint/fee PDAs `init`. Vault ATAs must pre-exist (client-created). OK.
- **`update_bucket_info`:** `has_one = creator`; name/thesis only; performance history untouched. OK.
- **`close_bucket`:** `has_one = creator`; blocks new mints, keeps redeem open, frees a creator slot; clears pending. `active_buckets -= 1` cannot underflow (an Open bucket implies it was incremented). OK.
- **`settle_commission` (permissionless):** values at min(spot,TWAP); mints fee tokens only if unit>HWM; ratchets HWM to the post-fee unit price (no double-charge); requires all-fresh. Idempotent (second call sees unit==HWM → no-op). Prunes only holdings with `weight==0 && balance==0 && reserved==0`. OK.
- **`claim_fees`:** recomputes the expected `creator_fee`/`platform_fee` PDA and checks it; creator path requires `signer==creator` and `destination.owner==creator`; platform path forces `destination.owner==config.fee_wallet` (anyone may trigger). Cannot mis-route fees. OK (existing test: creator can't claim platform's and vice-versa).
- **Fee custody design:** commission is minted into program-owned fee accounts, not the creator's wallet, so a creator cannot brick settlement by closing their token account. OK (good).

### money-in: `open_mint`, `fill_mint`, `close_mint_order`
- **`open_mint` signers/settlement:** backer signs; settles commission **first** (nobody enters ahead of a fee); charges mint fee (+ optional sponsor rent-fee) to fee wallet; escrows net USDC to an order-owned ATA; budgets legs by current value proportions (or target weights when supply==0) and snapshots `unit_price`. `rent_fee_e6 ≤ $1` and only when `payer != backer`; moves only the backer's own USDC, backer signs. OK.
- **Anti-dilution (reference price + `min()` + slippage):** each leg records its price at open; `fill_mint` credits `min(qty×ref_price, spent)/unit_price_open` tokens. A held stock **appreciating** after open makes the reference stale-low → the fill fails the slippage bound (cannot mint appreciation cheaply); a stock **falling** only costs the backer. Residual dilution is bounded by the slippage tolerance (≤1% + extra_cost) per leg. **Proven:** `review.test.ts` → `D1` (5% post-open rise ⇒ `SlippageExceeded`; 5% post-open fall ⇒ fills, vault grows). Unit test `mint_tokens_capped_by_spend` confirms the `min()` cap.
- **`fill_mint` CPI:** keeper **or** backer; order `has_one=bucket`; `swap_program` must be allow-listed; escrow (source) is order-owned and signed by the order PDA (which owns nothing else); `guard_swap_accounts(allowed=[holding.vault])`; post-swap checks `spent ≤ usdc_in`, escrow authority/delegate/close unchanged, `qty=vault_after-vault_before ≥ min_out>0` (output must land in the leg's vault), and `value_in` within slippage of `spent`. Diverting output to a non-vault account ⇒ `OutputTooLow`; spending more ⇒ `OverSpent`; over-crediting via qty inflation is capped by `min(,spent)`. OK (existing test: keeper cannot divert a mint leg).
- **`close_mint_order`:** refund goes only to `backer_usdc` (authority=backer); rent to `order.rent_payer`; `close = rent_payer` closes the order. Backer/keeper any time; anyone once done/expired. Nonce-seeded order → no reinit while open; reuse after close creates a fresh account. OK.

### money-out: `redeem`, `fill_redeem`, `claim_redeem_in_kind`, `close_redeem_order`
- **`redeem`:** holder signs; settles only if all-fresh (else `settled=false`, F2); burns `tokens - redeem_fee` from the holder; reserves `floor(available_i × burn / supply)` per holding (price-independent, rounds down in the vault's favour); sub-cent slices left with holders. Works when the bucket is closed. `reserved ≤ balance` invariant maintained (`available = balance - reserved`, and `floor(available×burn/supply) ≤ available` since `burn ≤ supply`). OK.
- **`fill_redeem` CPI:** keeper **or** holder; order `has_one=bucket`; allow-listed program; swap signed by the **bucket PDA** with `guard_swap_accounts(allowed=[holding.vault])`; `sold = vault_before-vault_after ≤ qty ≤ leg.remaining()`; `assert_no_authority_change` on the vault; USDC must reach `holder_usdc` (`token::authority=order.holder`); **keeper** path additionally enforces freshness + slippage vs on-chain price; holder path uses the holder's own `min_usdc_out`. Keeper gets nothing and cannot skim beyond slippage. **Proven:** `review.test.ts` → `D2` (keeper routing the reserved stock into another vault ⇒ `ForbiddenSwapAccount`; the reserved slice stays claimable in kind). Existing tests: keeper can't send proceeds to itself, can't touch another vault/mint/fee account, can't use a non-allow-listed program.
- **`claim_redeem_in_kind` (keeper-free exit):** holder signs; `has_one=bucket, holder`; vault + mint checked; transfers `leg.remaining()` from vault to the holder's own token account signed by the bucket PDA; needs no price/keeper/venue. Transfer-fee tokens: source is debited exactly `remaining` (holder eats the fee), so `reserved`/`balance` stay consistent. OK (existing stale-price test exercises the full keeper-free exit).
- **`close_redeem_order`:** permissionless once `all_done()`; rent to `order.rent_payer`. OK.

### edits/rebalance (phase 2): `propose_edit`, `admin_propose_removal`, `activate_edit`, `rebalance`
- **`propose_edit`:** `has_one=creator`; bucket Open; no pending edit; cooldown enforced; recipe re-validated (kept holdings may retain a since-disabled mint; new mints must be enabled). 24h delay recorded. OK.
- **`admin_propose_removal`:** admin only; removes a mint from the pending/active set, renormalizes to whole percent, same 24h notice, ignores cooldown. **Caveat (L2):** does not re-check per-type weight caps or `min_holdings`, so a forced removal can leave a holding above its cap or a bucket with a single holding. Admin action, delisting context — Low.
- **`activate_edit` (permissionless):** after the effective time; zeroes all weights then applies pending; new holdings require their canonical vault ATA (key + owner==bucket + mint match) in remaining accounts; caps total entries at `MAX_ENTRIES=24`. Wrong remaining accounts ⇒ error, no corruption. OK.
- **`rebalance`:** keeper only; both sides must be holdings; needs a recent valuation (≤1h) and fresh prices; must move from an **over-weight** to an **under-weight** holding, sized within the over-weight excess (`from_available = balance - reserved`, so reserved slices are protected); output lands back in the vault; within slippage (+ both extra_costs); guard rejects any non-`{from,to}` bucket-owned account; `assert_no_authority_change` on both vaults; prunes a fully-sold zero-weight source. Keeper gets nothing; theft bounded to slippage even with a malicious allow-listed swap. **L1:** no cooldown/rate-limit — a rogue **trusted** keeper can slowly erode vault value via repeated within-slippage rebalances (funds stay in the vault; the loss leaks to the venue as fees). Low. OK otherwise (existing tests: direction/excess/staleness enforced; can't route output out).

### Cross-cutting checks
- **Signer checks:** every state change has an appropriate `Signer`; permissionless instructions (`settle_commission`, `activate_edit`, `close_redeem_order`, and expired/`all_done` `close_mint_order`) only perform their intended, already-authorized transitions. OK.
- **Account ownership / discriminators:** typed `Account<T>`/`InterfaceAccount` everywhere for program/token accounts; raw reads (`read_token_account`, guard) verify owner ∈ {Token, Token-2022} and the SPL/Token-2022 account layout (`is_token_account`/`is_mint`) before slicing. OK.
- **PDA seeds/bumps & canonical addresses:** config/asset/creator/bucket/mint/fee/order PDAs seed-checked with stored bumps; vault ATAs pinned to `get_associated_token_address_with_program_id(bucket, mint, token_program)` at add time and `require_keys_eq!`-checked on every use; escrow is the order's USDC ATA. OK.
- **`has_one`/`address` constraints:** `has_one=bucket`/`creator`/`holder`, `address=bucket.token_mint`, `address=config.admin`, `address=order.rent_payer`, `token::authority`/`token::mint` constraints present on every relevant account. OK.
- **Remaining-accounts handling (`load_holdings`, `recipe::validate`, `activate_edit`, swap accounts):** length-checked; each `[asset_i, vault_i]` pair validated against the recorded mint/vault/owner; `Account::try_from` on assets is safe because assets are admin-only and uniquely keyed by mint (`asset.mint == holding.mint` pins to the one canonical account); swap remaining-accounts pass through `guard_swap_accounts`. OK.
- **Arithmetic:** `u128` intermediates; `overflow-checks = true` ⇒ overflow aborts (no wraparound); all `u128→u64` casts occur after range-limiting divisions; rounding always favours the vault/holders (fee tokens, redeem qty, sub-cent drop); no division by zero (`supply==0`/`unit==0`/`sum==0` guarded). Commission is exactly `20% × (U−H) × S` on the **conservative** unit price and can never exceed 20%; HWM ratchets to the post-fee unit price. **Proven:** unit tests `worked_example`, `commission_preserves_value_identity`, `no_commission_at_or_below_mark`, `redeem_is_pro_rata_and_rounds_down`, `split_is_exact`, and the on-chain worked-example suite. OK.
- **CPI targets & swap guard:** only `config.swap_programs` (admin-set) are callable; `guard_swap_accounts` rejects the bucket mint and every bucket-owned token account not in `allowed`; the bucket PDA may appear only as signer (runtime forbids the CPI writing to a program-owned account); balance-delta + `assert_no_authority_change` + slippage bound damage from an adversarial allow-listed swap to at most the slippage tolerance, with output forced back to the vault (fill_mint/rebalance) or to the holder (fill_redeem). OK.
- **Reentrancy:** the only CPI targets are the token program and the admin-allow-listed swap venue (Jupiter v6 on mainnet); Anchor reloads account state after each CPI; there is no callback into `bucket_vault`. Trust assumption: the allow-list contains only non-reentrant programs (enforced by admin). OK given trust model.
- **Reinit/close:** orders are nonce-seeded and `close`d to the rent payer; escrow closed via CPI; `init`/`init_if_needed` used correctly (no type confusion). OK.
- **State consistency (`reserved`, pruning, holdings vs remaining-accounts order):** `reserved ≤ balance` holds; pruning only removes holdings with `reserved==0 && balance==0 && weight==0`, so it can never drop a holding an open redeem order still owes (an open leg keeps `reserved>0`); `fill_redeem`/`claim_redeem_in_kind`/`fill_mint` resolve the holding by `find(mint)` (order-independent); the SDK rebuilds `[asset,vault]` lists from current holdings so a mismatch after a prune fails safely rather than corrupting. OK.
- **Token-2022 edge cases:** holdings use `token_interface`/`transfer_checked`; per-asset `extra_cost_bps` widens the slippage bound for issuer transfer fees; withheld fees accrue only to the issuer (a disclosed leak, not a program bug); scaled-UI/splits handled off-chain via `price_raw = price_ui × multiplier` + `force_price`, bounded on-chain by the ±20% clamp; frozen/paused tokens make transfers fail but redeem still burns+reserves so the claim survives the pause; transfer hooks are currently null and would need SDK-supplied extra metas if set. Permanent delegate / freeze authority are issuer powers the program cannot prevent. See residual risks R2. OK (as designed/disclosed).
- **Price manipulation:** ±20% clamp + TWAP + min(spot,TWAP) conservative valuation; stale (>1h) prices block mints and keeper sells but never redeem; reference price fixed at order open. A one-push spike pays no commission (proven: `rules.test.ts` price-feed suite). OK.
- **Economic:** sandwich/slippage bounded by `max_slippage_bps (+extra_cost)`; failed/illiquid legs are abandoned and refunded; dust legs fill or are dropped; mint+redeem same slot only costs fees/swap; F1 (concentration via dust-funding) and F2 (stale-redeem commission escape) as above; rebalance churn (L1). OK except F1/F2/L1.

## Residual risks for the external audit

- **R1 — Privileged keys (centralization).** `admin` can (a) `set_roles` to swap in a **malicious swap program**, and (b) `force_price` to inflate valuation and mint fake commission (dilution). `price_authority` can move valuation within the ±20%/push band. **None of these can directly withdraw vault assets** — the swap guard + balance-delta + slippage checks bound even a malicious allow-listed swap to the slippage tolerance, and redeem is price-independent — but a compromised admin/price key can dilute holders via bogus commission and degrade execution. Move `admin` to a multisig before mainnet (per docs/decisions.md), keep `price_authority` isolated and monitored, and alert on `force_price`, `set_roles`, and any change to `swap_programs`. Confirm the mainnet allow-list is exactly Jupiter v6 and that Jupiter shared-accounts routing behaves under the guard on real xStocks/PreStocks mints (untested on mainnet per the token-programs spike).
- **R2 — Token-2022 issuer powers.** xStocks/PreStocks carry a **permanent delegate** and **freeze/pausable** authority (issuer): the issuer can move or freeze vault-held tokens, and `assert_no_authority_change` cannot detect a permanent-delegate move (it lives on the mint, not the account). Withheld transfer fees accrue to the issuer. Scaled-UI multipliers (a 5× SpaceX split is scheduled) must be applied correctly off-chain or a split reads as a price jump (clamped, but confirm the `force_price` reset procedure). These are inherent asset-level trust assumptions and belong in risk disclosures + counsel's review of issuer terms.
- **R3 — F1 economic gating.** The creator-funding minimum is enforceable only weakly (Medium finding). Decide whether it should gate on filled value; also consider a per-mint max deviation from target weights so a griefing creator cannot over-concentrate the first backer.
- **R4 — F2 / feed-outage commission.** Accepted trade-off; confirm the product is comfortable that commission is skipped for anyone exiting during a price-feed outage, and set a feed-uptime SLA + staleness alerting.
- **R5 — Reentrancy / venue trust.** The whole custody argument for redeem/rebalance rests on the allow-list containing only well-behaved (non-reentrant, honest-price) programs. This is a trust boundary, not a code-enforced one; the audit should confirm Jupiter v6 cannot be driven to re-enter or to satisfy the delta checks while skimming beyond slippage on real routes.
- **R6 — Compute/liveness at scale.** 15-holding mints span multiple transactions and a 15→15 edit cannot activate until old holdings are sold below `MAX_ENTRIES=24`; and a fully-redeemed vault that still holds fee tokens blocks new mints (`ZeroVaultValue`) until the fee tokens are redeemed to zero supply. Liveness edges, not custody risks; worth an operational runbook.
